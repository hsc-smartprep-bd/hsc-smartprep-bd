About HSC SmartPrep BD

Welcome to HSC SmartPrep BD, a study resource created to help Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) students in Bangladesh prepare for their examinations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make exam preparation more accessible by providing practice questions, subject-based revision, mock tests, and tools to help students track their learning progress.

What We Offer

  • MCQ practice for science subjects and ICT
  • Subject-based revision and self-assessment
  • Mock tests for exam preparation
  • Learning progress tracking

Our Commitment

We aim to improve our educational resources over time. Students should verify important academic information against their official textbooks and curriculum.

HSC SmartPrep BD is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with any education board.
